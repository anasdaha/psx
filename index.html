How this is worked out

  • Stocks: the KSE-100 index members listed on dps.psx.com.pk. Sharia-compliant means the stock is also in PSX's KMI All Share index.
  • Today compares the latest price with the previous close. Week compares with the close 5 trading sessions back, Month with the close 21 sessions back.
  • During market hours the price is PSX's latest traded price at the last sync, so it moves until the close.
  • Volume is shares traded over the same period. A "thin" tag means volume was under a quarter of the typical KSE-100 stock for that period, so the move rests on few trades.
  • An "XD" tag means the stock went ex-dividend in the period. Prices are not adjusted, so its Week or Month change is understated by the dividend.
  • Predictions use a multi-factor score. Twenty factors were tested on 13 months of PSX prices (Sep 2025 to Oct 2026), and five were kept: low volatility, small daily ranges, a recent fall compared with the sector, no recent one-day spike, and closeness to the 52-week high. Buying recent winners (momentum) and following trends did not work on PSX, so the score does not use them.
  • Each factor is ranked within the stock's group (Sharia or non-Sharia), and the score is the weighted average of those ranks, shown from 0 to 100. Swing is the average gap between a day's high and low, as a share of the price, over the last 20 days. "From high" is how far the price is below its highest point of the past year.
  • The track record shown with the picks is recalculated every day on the past year of data. The score's edge is small: its picks beat the typical stock a little more often than chance, but their average return is close to the group average.
  • Every prediction is saved, one file per trading day, and kept for 90 days. The version saved after the close is the final one. Once its period has passed (1, 5 or 21 trading days), each pick is graded on PSX closing prices with the same test as the track record: did it beat the typical (median) stock in its group? Until then, Past predictions shows results so far from the latest prices.
  • The track record uses today's KSE-100 members, so it leaves out companies that dropped out of the index. That makes past results look a little better than they really were.
  • Nothing here is a buy or sell recommendation.
